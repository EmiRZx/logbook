00012026-08–09

Kestrel-49M

Global Innovation Build Challenge V2, Track 01

A 49.5M-parameter language model trained from random initialization on a 6B-token corpus I built, with its own tokenizer, in 2.08 H100-hours.

  • Pretrained a 49.5M-parameter Llama-style decoder from scratch under a 50M cap that counted embeddings, on a 6B-token corpus and 16k BPE tokenizer I built.
  • Chose the model shape with a 7-way parameter-matched sweep; the usual deep-and-thin advice cost 64% more GPU-hours for a worse loss.
  • Outscored GPT-2-124M on ARC-Easy (0.4217 vs 0.3948, paired bootstrap p < 0.001) at 2.5× fewer parameters, for about $14 of rented GPU in total.
  • Repository, as submitted
  • Released weights
